This Privacy Policy explains how CLAM ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the CLAM iOS application (the "App").

Important Commitment: CLAM does not send your personal data, photos, images containing faces, prompts, or generated content to any third-party AI service providers. All AI-related processing is performed on your device or on our own servers under our direct control.

Face Data Summary

Our App processes images that may contain facial information for AI generation features. Face data collection, use, disclosure, sharing, storage, and retention are explained in Section 1.2 Face Data, with additional details in Section 4 (Third-Party Services and Data Sharing), Section 6 (Data Storage and Retention), and Section 7 (Your Choices and Rights).

1. Information We Collect

1.1 General Data

  • Account and session data: anonymous account identifiers, authentication/session tokens, and related status data needed to keep you signed in and deliver app features.
  • Device and app data: basic device/app metadata and app event data used for analytics and attribution.
  • Purchase data: subscription and consumable purchase information needed to verify purchases and update subscription/credit status.
  • User content: photos/videos you choose to upload for AI generation (which may contain faces), text prompts you provide, and generated outputs returned to you.
  • Feedback data: information you submit through in-app feedback/contact channels.

1.2 Face Data

When you use CLAM AI generation features, the App may process images that contain facial information. We collect and use face data only as follows:

  • What we collect (collection): Images you upload or capture that may contain your face or the face of others, solely to provide the feature you request.
  • How we use it (use): Face data is used exclusively to perform requested AI generation. We do not use face data for identity recognition, biometric identification, profiling, or advertising.
  • Disclosure and sharing: We do not disclose or share face data with any third-party AI providers. Face data is processed only on your device or on our own servers.
  • Storage: If server-side processing is needed, face data (as part of uploaded media) is stored only on infrastructure directly controlled by us and not transmitted to external AI providers.
  • Retention: Face data processed on our servers is automatically deleted within 7 days after processing completion. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 7); deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

Face data collection, use, disclosure, sharing, storage, and retention are explained in this section and in Section 4, Section 6, and Section 7.

2. Permissions We Use

  • Camera: to capture content when you choose to use camera-based features.
  • Photo Library (Read): to let you select existing media.
  • Photo Library (Add): to save generated media to your device.
  • Tracking (ATT): permission is requested under Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework for attribution/measurement where applicable.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide core app functions, including media upload, AI task creation, and result delivery.
  • Authenticate users, maintain sessions, and protect service security.
  • Process and verify subscriptions and credits through Apple's in-app purchase systems.
  • Measure app performance, installation/purchase attribution, and improve product quality.
  • Respond to support or feedback requests.

4. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

CLAM does not use any third-party AI services. No user data (photos, images containing faces, prompts, or generated content) is sent to external AI providers.

We may use non-AI third-party service providers to operate the App, including:

  • Apple (StoreKit): in-app purchase processing and receipt-related verification workflows.
  • Meta/Facebook SDK: analytics and attribution-related app events.
  • Backend infrastructure providers: account/session handling, task processing, and content-related service operations.

These providers process only the data necessary for their services and do not receive face data for AI processing.

We do not share user media or face data with third-party AI providers.

5. Tracking and ATT

Where required, tracking-related data access depends on your ATT choice. If you decline tracking permission, attribution capabilities may be limited, but core app functionality remains available.

6. Data Storage and Retention

  • Authentication tokens may be stored securely on device (for example, in iOS Keychain).
  • Certain app state/preferences may be stored locally (for example, in UserDefaults).
  • Uploaded and generated media may be processed/stored on backend services as needed to provide features.

We retain information only as long as necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Face data and media retention: Images and videos containing facial information that are processed for AI features are automatically deleted from our servers within 7 days after processing completion. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 7), and we will process deletion requests within 30 days.
